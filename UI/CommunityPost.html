Hi Mike,

I've been using the CableMod 12VHPWR cable (not the adapter, but the full replacement cable for Corsair PSUs) for about 6 months now on my 4090 without any issues. The key is to make sure it's fully seated until you hear a distinct "click" and there's no gap between the connector and the GPU header.

Regarding the 90-degree adapters specifically, there is an active recall/replacement program for the V1.0 adapters. If you buy one now, make sure it's the V1.1 version which has fixed the sense pin issues.

Also, since you have a Corsair PSU, I'd highly recommend just getting their official 12VHPWR cable. It's much more flexible than the NVIDIA octopus adapter and eliminates failure points.
