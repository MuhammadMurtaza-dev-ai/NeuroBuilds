GeForce RTX 4080 Founders Edition Review

Is the RTX 4080 the sweet spot for 4K gaming, or is it overshadowed by its bigger brother? We benchmark 50+ games to find out.

person
Alex Chen
Senior Hardware Editor
October 22, 2023
Updated: 2 days ago

The GeForce RTX 4080 has arrived, occupying that awkward middle ground between the "affordable" high-end and the absolute pinnacle of performance that is the RTX 4090. With a price tag of $1,199, it's not exactly a budget option, but it promises significant gains over the previous generation's 3080 Ti.

In this review, we're taking a deep dive into the Founders Edition model, looking at its architectural improvements, thermal performance, and of course, raw gaming power across a variety of resolutions.

Ada Lovelace Architecture

Built on TSMC's 4N process, the AD103 silicon powering the RTX 4080 is a marvel of efficiency. It features 9,728 CUDA cores, significantly fewer than the 4090's 16,384, but clocks higher out of the box. The inclusion of 3rd Gen RT Cores and 4th Gen Tensor Cores brings DLSS 3 to the forefront, a feature NVIDIA is banking on heavily.

"DLSS 3 Frame Generation is a game changer for CPU-bound scenarios, essentially creating frames out of thin air with minimal latency penalties thanks to NVIDIA Reflex."

Gaming Performance (4K)

At 4K resolution with Ray Tracing enabled, the RTX 4080 delivers playable frame rates without upscaling, but it truly shines when DLSS is activated. In Cyberpunk 2077, with RT Overdrive mode, we saw average FPS jump from 28 to over 90 with DLSS 3 Performance mode.

Game Title Resolution RTX 3080 Ti RTX 4080 Diff
Cyberpunk 2077 4K Ultra (No DLSS) 22 FPS 31 FPS +41%
Call of Duty: MW2 4K Extreme 78 FPS 112 FPS +43%
Shadow of TR 4K Highest 95 FPS 138 FPS +45%

Thermal Analysis

The Founders Edition cooler is frankly over-engineered for the AD103 chip. It shares the same physical dimensions as the 4090 cooler but has to dissipate significantly less heat (320W TGP vs 450W). As a result, the card runs exceptionally cool and quiet. Under full Furmark load for 30 minutes, the GPU core never exceeded 64°C with fans spinning at a barely audible 1300 RPM.

Verdict

8.5

Excellent Performance, Premium Price

The RTX 4080 is a fantastic piece of engineering that is unfortunately held back by its aggressive pricing. If you can stomach the cost, it's a 4K monster that runs cool and quiet.
